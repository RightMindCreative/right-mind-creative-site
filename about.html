02 / Meet the founder

Ryan “Roo”
Vandergriend.

Roo’s journey in music began early—writing songs at five and sitting in on studio sessions by eleven. Over the past 15 years, he has refined his craft independently and alongside local and national artists.

Right Mind Creative grew from a clear gap in Omaha’s creative scene: abundant talent, but too little professional, reliable support. Driven by frustration and belief in that potential, Roo set out to build something different.

His approach starts with a simple truth: no two artists are the same. Instead of forcing a formula, he helps each person discover, refine, and develop a sound that feels authentic.
